Privacy Policy

Aura Sparepart Poster
Last updated: November 21, 2026

Aura Sparepart Poster ("the App") is a private tool used by Aura Sparepart to publish and manage videos on its own TikTok account, @aura_sparepart_. This policy explains what data the App uses.

1. Data we access

With the account owner's permission, given through TikTok Login, the App accesses:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (display name, avatar, account ID)
  • The account's own public videos, to list and manage posts
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to the account

2. How we use it

This data is used only to publish and manage videos on the Aura Sparepart TikTok account. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

3. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok, through its official APIs, and with the service providers needed to run the connection. It is not shared with anyone else.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by the connection service and are used only for the purposes above. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings, under Security → Manage app permissions.

5. Other users

The App does not collect data from other TikTok users or the public.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions can be sent through the TikTok account @aura_sparepart_.
